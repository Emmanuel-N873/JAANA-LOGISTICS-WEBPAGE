About JAANA Logistics

JAANA Logistics (JAANA Delivery Services) is a customer-focused logistics and delivery company based in Lagos, Nigeria. We provide fast, reliable, secure and affordable delivery solutions to individuals, businesses and organisations.

Our mission is to provide accessible, efficient and dependable logistics solutions that simplify deliveries, support business growth and give customers confidence in every shipment.

What we deliver

Documents, e-commerce parcels, food orders, bulk business shipments and custom logistics arrangements.
